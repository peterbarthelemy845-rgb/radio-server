# Sponsor / AdSense setup

Current mode: DEMO ONLY. No Google advertising scripts, cookies, publisher IDs, ad requests, paid impressions, or revenue. The radio-add placement is revealed only when /api/radio/commands/<id> returns added. The station-owner placement appears only after successful email verification publishes a new station; pending moderation, invalid codes, and editor sign-in never show it. /sponsor-preview is a read-only, noindex design preview and never sends radio commands.

When an AdSense account is approved, obtain the actual publisher ID (ca-pub-...) and responsive display ad slot ID from that account. Do not substitute placeholder IDs or enable Auto ads site-wide. Before activation, review placement eligibility (particularly completion pages), consent/CMP requirements for visitor regions, privacy disclosures, and CSP permissions. If this completion page is not eligible, keep a direct sponsor here and use Google ads on eligible content pages instead. ads.txt must use the actual account-provided record; no fake record is served now. Real ad integration remains disabled until those prerequisites are available. Never require watching or clicking an ad to add a station.
