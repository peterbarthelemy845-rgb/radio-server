# Local radio sponsor ads — free pilot

Station owners sign in through the existing mailbox-verification flow, then choose Manage radio ads in /station/edit. No ordinary user accounts were reintroduced. /station/ads requires a current, station-scoped verified session; /local-ads-demo is a read-only example without payment or device actions.

There are no charges, Stripe API calls, checkout buttons, subscription entitlements, or payment credentials. Owners collect their own advertisers' payments separately. The built-in La Voix Divine station is managed by a full administrator at /admin/local-ads/main; this does not give owners access to other stations. Before charging a fee later, agree on a price and cancellation/refund policy, connect an appropriate Stripe account, create hosted Checkout subscriptions and Customer Portal sessions server-side, and verify signed, replay-protected webhooks before enabling any paid entitlement. Never unlock access from a browser's success URL. Use test mode before live billing. No Stripe account/key is needed for this pilot.

## Screening and control
Images are decoded, bounded and normalized to new private PNGs. The existing OpenAI artwork scanner checks names/messages/images using the existing configured key. Locally flagged, unscanned, failed or suspicious results wait for a full administrator at /admin/local-ads. Approval does not start a campaign: the owner must explicitly choose Start. Pause/delete, dates, station suspension and the administrator's global switch block new delivery. Ads are scoped to the station whose mailbox grant is current; neither public station codes nor Device Codes authorize owner changes.

Uploads are images only, not executable HTML, remote URLs, video or audio. Up to twenty undeleted ads per station. Text and artwork are immutable; replace an ad by pausing/deleting it and uploading a new version. Schedules use America/New_York local dates, including daylight saving changes. The owner confirms advertising/image rights. This technical screening is not a guarantee that advertising claims are lawful or accurate.

## Devices and measurement
Updated firmware waits for ten uninterrupted listening minutes before requesting a sponsor and waits thirty minutes between popups. The popup has a close button, closes automatically in eight seconds, preserves audio and avoids setup, pairing, standby and warning screens. Delivery requires the radio's existing private credential and verified HTTPS. The 256x76 RGB565 image is bounded to 38,912 bytes. Signed short-lived media grants are tied to the device, receipt and campaign revision; the credential is still required to download. Display acknowledgements are idempotent, authenticated and bounded. They indicate device-reported displays, not human attention, unique viewers, sales or third-party-certified advertising impressions. Counts cannot prevent a compromised device from lying.

Local campaign metadata and receipt tables live in the existing persistent owners database. Images live in the existing backed-up private artwork folder. Restoring a backup pauses campaigns, switches delivery off, and drops old display receipts/cooldowns, preventing stale delivery replay. No ad data appears in public station metadata or website analytics. Existing firmware ignores this feature until an updated sketch is uploaded.
