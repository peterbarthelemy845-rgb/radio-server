Administrator checklist

Review new requests promptly. For a valid takedown, identify and disable the correct listing/artwork and promptly notify the station owner. Record your evidence and notification details below. Check counter-notice validity, forwarding and the applicable 10–14 business-day restoration procedure with counsel; the site does not calculate legal deadlines or restore automatically.

Terminate submission access for repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances. Do not treat an unverified accusation as a final finding. Termination here disables all listings for that registered email and blocks new submissions from it. Review related identities manually.

Still required outside this website: legal review of the agreement, confirmation of the business operator/contact details, and designated-agent registration/publication if seeking DMCA protections. A complaint form alone does not establish eligibility.

Device limitation: suspended listings are removed from Library and code lookups. Older/offline radios may continue playing saved direct streams; firmware enforcement and third-party stream removal are separate tasks.
