# Monthly station plans — preview only

Chosen tiers: 0–10 distinct active radios per New York calendar month free; 11–100 $30/month; 101–300 $50/month; 301+ Contact us. Station ad tools have no separate owner fee. Owner editor shows proposed prices and aggregate reported usage. No Stripe, checkout, payment credentials, invoices, paid entitlements, automatic upgrades or playback restrictions exist. A future payment agreement must be explicitly accepted; the table is not an invoice.

Updated firmware reports playing_station_code and playing_confirmed=true inside its existing authenticated radio poll after sixty uninterrupted playing seconds. Server checks the radio credential, validates the station code and current publication, and writes a unique (month, station key, monthly HMAC device hash) record in radio-links.sqlite3, already covered by existing backups. Repeated polls and station re-selection do not increase that month's station count. Another station or month counts independently. Retention is current plus twelve previous calendar months, pruned on new activity. Older firmware is excluded; zero reports is not evidence of zero actual listeners. Owners cannot see individual IDs or change counts through public station codes.

DO NOT ENABLE BILLING FROM THIS PREVIEW. Device identities are currently self-provisioned; a malicious client can register synthetic radios or lie about playback. Before charging, introduce trusted factory provisioning/attestation or an equivalent reviewed trust model, invalid-report detection and reconciliation. Factory reset/rekey or server signing-key rotation can alter deduplication; handle migrations before billing. A shared radio can represent several people and direct-stream listeners on other players are not counted. These are device-reported activity counts only, not concurrent listeners, certified audience figures or music royalty reporting.

After payment-provider setup, define the actual billing cycle, how upgrades/overages work, cancellation/refunds, taxes, notice and customer consent. Test payment webhooks and entitlement transitions before enabling any charge or limit. Usage recording errors are logged without private contents and cannot break phone pairing or station command delivery.
