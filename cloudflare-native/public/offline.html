Wi-Fi setup help
  1. On your radio, open Settings, then Wi-Fi.
  2. Choose your 2.4 GHz network. Scan refreshes the list; Type name lets you enter a hidden network.
  3. Enter your Wi-Fi password on the radio, then tap Connect.
  4. If you use phone setup, join Radio-Setup, then open the local setup page below.
