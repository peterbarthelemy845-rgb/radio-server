Add to your radio

Keep your radio on and connected to Wi-Fi.

Connect your radio using your phone.

  1. Turn on your radio

    Wait for Radio-Setup to appear in your phone’s Wi-Fi list.

  2. Join Radio-Setup

    No password is needed. Stay connected if your phone says “No Internet.”

  3. Choose your home Wi-Fi

    Open setup below, choose your 2.4 GHz network, enter its password, and tap Save.

Open Wi-Fi setup192.168.4.1

When your radio connects, return your phone to its usual Wi-Fi.

Radio-Setup not showing?

Use your radio’s screen: Settings → Wi-Fi. Choose your network, enter its password, and tap Connect.

To replace saved Wi-Fi using your phone, hold the station knob for 5 seconds. This clears the saved Wi-Fi.

Still not opening? While joined to Radio-Setup, enter 192.168.4.1 in your browser.
